# Viona Intro Reel — "The Before/After Smackdown"

**Format:** 9:16 vertical (Instagram Reels / TikTok / Shorts)
**Duration:** ~50 seconds
**Music:** Dark, building electronic beat that drops at the transformation moment
**Audience:** Solo creators (YouTubers, educators, course creators)
**Tone:** Hype & bold, product launch energy
**Core Message:** Viona makes visuals for you — AI auto-generates graphics synced to your narration

---

## SECTION 1: THE HOOK (0:00 - 0:05)

**VISUAL:** A generic, flat talking-head video. No graphics. No subtitles. Just someone explaining something to camera. Looks... fine. Boring.

**TEXT ON SCREEN (bold, glitchy):**
> "This is your explainer video."

**SFX:** Dull, flat tone.

**SPEAK** *(tone: flat, unimpressed)*

> "This is what most explainer videos look like. Just you... talking."

---

## SECTION 2: THE PAIN (0:05 - 0:12)

**VISUAL:** Quick cuts of the painful editing process — dragging shapes in Canva, scrubbing through a timeline, googling "how to make animated diagrams", stock footage searches returning garbage.

**TEXT ON SCREEN (rapid, stacking):**
> 3 HOURS. STILL BORING.

**SFX:** Chaotic, glitchy cuts.

**SPEAK** *(tone: frustrated, speeding up)*

> "And then you spend three hours dragging shapes in Canva, syncing graphics by hand, styling subtitles frame by frame — and it STILL looks mid."

---

## SECTION 3: THE PIVOT (0:12 - 0:16)

**VISUAL:** Black screen. Beat drops.

**TEXT ON SCREEN (massive, centered):**
> "Now watch this."

**SFX:** Bass drop / impact hit.

**SPEAK** *(tone: dead calm, confident)*

> "So we built something."

*(2 second pause — let the beat drop do the work)*

---

## SECTION 4: THE TRANSFORMATION (0:16 - 0:38)

**VISUAL:** The SAME talking-head video — but now processed through Viona. Rapid showcase of:

1. **(0:16-0:22)** Animated subtitles appearing word-by-word, styled in a bold MrBeast/Hormozi preset. Text pops, glows, scales.
2. **(0:22-0:30)** AI-generated visuals appearing in sync with the narration — a flowchart materializes as the speaker explains a process, a comparison graphic slides in during a "versus" moment, data animates into a clean chart.
3. **(0:30-0:38)** Split-screen / PIP layout shift — the talking head shrinks to a corner while full-screen animated diagrams take over. Everything is perfectly synced to the speaker's words.

**TEXT ON SCREEN (key moments):**
> "AI-GENERATED VISUALS"
> "SYNCED TO YOUR VOICE"
> "ZERO EDITING"

**SPEAK** *(tone: building hype, each line lands harder)*

> "This is Viona. You give it your video — and it generates the visuals FOR you."
>
> "It listens to what you're saying and creates diagrams, charts, and animations — synced perfectly to your words."
>
> "Subtitles? Styled automatically. Layouts? Done. You didn't touch an editor. Not once."

**VISUAL:** Side-by-side comparison — left: the boring original, right: the Viona version. Night and day.

**TEXT ON SCREEN (bold, clean):**

**VISUAL:** Viona logo + website (viona.app) fade in.

---

## DELIVERY TIPS

- **Sections 1-2:** Talk like you're venting to a creator friend. Casual frustration.
- **Section 3:** Drop your energy completely. One calm line. The contrast makes it hit.
- **Section 4:** Let your voice build with each sentence. By "not once" you should sound like you're revealing a secret.
- **Section 5:** Slow down. Let the tagline breathe. This is the line people remember.

---

## PRODUCTION NOTES

- **Music cue:** Needs a clear build-to-drop at 0:12-0:16 for the pivot moment
- **Key edit technique:** Fast cuts in sections 1-2 (chaos energy), smooth/slick in section 4 (contrast = credibility)
- **Subtitles on the reel itself:** Use Viona's own subtitle styles as a meta-flex
- **CTA:** Keep it one action — "link in bio" or a handle tag, not both
- **Total spoken time:** ~45 seconds. Leaves ~5 seconds of breathing room for cuts, music, and logo outro
