# Editing Style: B-Roll Focused

> Use this style when the video benefits from real-world visuals — product demos, travel, lifestyle, interviews, vlogs.

## Core Approach
The speaker is the anchor. Stock footage and images provide visual context and variety. No custom motion graphics — all visuals come from stock libraries (Pexels, Unsplash, Getty).

## Technique Selection

### Primary Techniques
- **B-roll stock footage** — fullscreen replacement at transcript-matched moments. Search for footage that matches the specific topic being discussed, not generic filler.
- **Photo overlays** — images overlaid on speaker (speaker still visible). Use for references, examples, screenshots.
- **Ken Burns pan/zoom** — apply to ALL static images. Never show a motionless image.
- **Punch-in on speaker** — at vocal emphasis moments. Crop to ~130-150% of original frame, centered on face.
- **Jump cuts** — remove all filler words, silences >750ms, retakes.

### Secondary Techniques
- **Heading/topic text** — at section transitions, top of frame.
- **Lower thirds** — for speaker name at start, topic labels at section changes.
- **Crossfade transitions** — between speaker and B-roll. Default 300ms.
- **SFX** — whoosh on B-roll entrances, subtle pop on text appearances.
- **Background music** — low energy underneath speech, higher during B-roll segments.
- **Audio ducking** — music drops to 15-20% during speech, rises to 50-60% during B-roll.

### Do NOT Use
- Custom Remotion scene animations
- Kinetic typography
- Glitch/RGB split transitions
- Screen shake
- Emoji/sticker overlays

## Pacing Rules
- B-roll clip duration: 3-8 seconds per clip
- Never more than 15 seconds of continuous speaker without a visual change
- B-roll should cover 30-50% of total video duration
- Punch-ins: 1-3 per minute, at natural emphasis points
- Heading text: hold for 2-4 seconds

## Display Modes
- B-roll: fullscreen (speaker audio continues underneath)
- Photo overlays: 40-60% of screen, positioned away from speaker's face
- Speaker: default framing or punch-in, never split-screen

## B-Roll Search Strategy
- Search based on the specific noun/verb/concept the speaker mentions
- Prefer video clips over static images (2:1 ratio)
- Avoid generic "business people in meeting" type footage
- Match the energy — calm topic = slow footage, excited topic = dynamic footage
