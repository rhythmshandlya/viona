# Visual Story Plan: "High and Low"

## Transcript Analysis

**Content:** "High and low." (3 words, 3.06 seconds)
**Core Concept:** Contrast, duality, range, extremes
**Narrative Arc:** Simple but powerful statement about opposing states

This ultra-short content requires maximum visual impact in minimal time. The beauty lies in the simplicity - showing the fundamental concept of contrast that exists everywhere in life, from emotions to physical positions to values.

## Story Arc

Given the brevity, the narrative structure is:
1. **ESTABLISHMENT** - Present the "high" state with visual drama
2. **CONNECTION** - The word "and" bridges the concepts
3. **CONTRAST** - Reveal the "low" state, completing the duality

## Visual Metaphor System

**Primary Metaphor:** A luminous geometric sphere that embodies position and state
- The sphere represents any measurable quantity or state
- Its vertical position literally demonstrates "high" vs "low"
- Color temperature shifts from warm (high energy) to cool (low energy)
- Scale changes to reinforce the concept (large=high, small=low)

**Color Palette:** Electric Sunset
- Primary: #ff6b6b (Coral) - for high energy moments
- Secondary: #feca57 (Gold) - for transitions
- Accent: #667eea (Cool Blue) - for low/calm states
- Dark: #1a1a2e - for depth and contrast

## Visual Continuity

**Persistent Element:** The sphere is the hero throughout
- Same geometric form maintains visual thread
- Position, color, and scale transform to show the concept
- Particle trail creates fluid motion between states

## Responsive Layout

**Canvas Utilization:** Full 1080x1920px with dramatic vertical movement
- High state uses upper 80% of screen
- Low state uses lower 20% of screen
- Typography scales to 6% of canvas height for mobile readability
- 10% safe margins maintained throughout

## Technical Requirements

- **No 3D Required** - All effects achievable with 2D transforms and particles
- **Icons Needed:** None - pure geometric abstraction
- **Spring Physics:** Standard config for sphere bounce and settle
- **Particles:** Simple radial emission system for energy visualization
