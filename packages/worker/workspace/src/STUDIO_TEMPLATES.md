## Available Templates by Category

### Timeline & Process (4)
- `agenda-tracker`: Video outline showing progress through agenda items
- `process-flow`: Step-by-step workflow with animated connecting arrows — ideal for funnels, how-to guides, and onboarding flows
- `step-counter`: Step indicator with progress ring for tutorials and how-tos
- `timeline-cascade`: Vertical timeline with animated milestone nodes — ideal for company history, project roadmaps, and event sequences

### Intro & Outro (8)
- `alert-banner`: Full-width alert banner with icon and attention-grabbing color
- `channel-intro`: Animated channel branding intro with floating geometric elements
- `chapter-card`: Full-screen chapter transition with number and title
- `countdown-reveal`: Top 5 countdown with dramatic item reveals — perfect for rankings, product launches, and listicles
- `credits-roll`: Classic scrolling credits with roles and names
- `end-screen`: YouTube-style end screen with video placeholders and subscribe button
- `guest-intro-card`: Guest introduction card with avatar, name, title, and bio
- `logo-stinger`: Branded logo reveal with geometric motion graphics — perfect for video intros, outros, and bumpers

### Social & Engagement (10)
- `audience-question`: Bold question prompt to drive viewer engagement and comments
- `comment-highlight`: Social media comment bubble overlay with avatar and reactions
- `coupon-badge`: Discount code stamp with shimmer effect
- `emoji-burst`: Floating emoji reactions rising from bottom of screen
- `emoji-slider-poll`: Instagram-style emoji slider poll with animated result
- `follower-milestone`: Animated subscriber count reaching a milestone with celebration effects
- `link-callout`: Animated callout with arrow pointing to link or resource
- `qr-code-reveal`: Animated QR code that builds itself with scan prompt
- `social-handle-bar`: Animated social media handles with platform icons
- `subscribe-nudge`: Animated subscribe, like, and bell notification overlay

### Data Visualization (11)
- `bar-chart-race`: Ranked horizontal bars that reorder over time — perfect for GDP rankings, brand battles, and trending data
- `number-ticker`: Large animated counter with prefix and suffix overlay
- `rating-display`: Animated star rating with score counter and review count
- `score-meter`: Animated semicircular gauge with sweeping needle and score
- `split-stat`: Side-by-side stat comparison with VS divider and fill bars
- `stat-bar-chart`: Horizontal bars growing with staggered spring animation — ideal for channel breakdowns and rankings
- `stat-comparison`: Two numbers side-by-side with animated arrow and change indicator — perfect for year-over-year and before/after comparisons
- `stat-counter`: Big animated number counting up with trend badge — perfect for revenue, users, or growth metrics
- `stat-donut`: SVG donut chart with animated arc drawing — great for revenue breakdowns and distribution data
- `stat-line-chart`: SVG line graph drawn progressively with gradient fill — perfect for growth trends and time series
- `stat-progress`: Multiple progress bars filling with staggered spring animation — ideal for objectives, KPIs, and completion tracking

### Comparison & Versus (5)
- `before-after-reveal`: Split-screen before vs after comparison with animated wipe divider — perfect for transformations, results, and growth stories
- `poll-battle`: Animated poll results racing to fill — options compete visually with a winner crowned at the end
- `pros-cons`: Two-column pros and cons comparison with animated icons
- `tier-board`: Animated tier list ranking with items snapping into S/A/B/C/D rows — viral format for rankings and opinions
- `versus-screen`: Dramatic split-screen VS comparison with attributes

### Text & Typography (7)
- `bullet-stack`: Cascading bullet-point list with animated checkmarks
- `definition-tooltip`: Dictionary-style tooltip with term, pronunciation, and definition
- `formula-display`: Mathematical formula with step-by-step reveal animation
- `headline-storm`: Text match cut effect — rapidly cycling news articles with the brand name always at center, highlighted
- `keyword-pop`: Bold keyword slam animation with impact effect
- `kinetic-caption`: Word-by-word animated captions with highlight effects
- `news-ticker`: Scrolling broadcast-style ticker tape with breaking badge

### Geographic & Maps (3)
- `country-highlight`: Highlight a country on a satellite map with animated zoom, colored overlay, and location labels
- `location-tag`: Map pin location overlay with name and coordinates
- `watercolor-map`: Animated travel route between two locations on a watercolor-style map with markers and a connecting line

### Social (5)
- `event-announce`: Bold event announcement with date, location, and call-to-action
- `quote-pulse`: Kinetic typography with dramatic word-by-word reveals — perfect for quotes, taglines, and key messages
- `speaker-id`: Animated lower-third name tag with name, title, and affiliation
- `testimonial-card`: Animated testimonial/review card with quote, rating, and author
- `topic-title`: Bold animated section title with subtitle and accent underline

### Education (3)
- `fact-flash`: Rapid-fire animated facts with big numbers and context — perfect for 'Did You Know' content and industry stats
- `popup-fact`: Notification-style popup cards with surprising facts
- `source-cite`: Editorial reference card for citing sources and quotes

### Marketing (2)
- `feature-list`: Cascading feature list with icons and descriptions
- `product-card`: Floating product showcase card with price, rating, and description

### Entertainment (2)
- `glitch-transition`: Digital glitch effect with RGB split, scan lines, and corruption blocks
- `swipe-wipe`: Bold geometric shape transition wipe with optional text

### Non-Card Templates (3)
- `path-draw-reveal`: SVG path drawing with progressive node reveal — use for processes, connections, step-by-step flows. NOT a card.
- `animated-diagram`: Node-and-edge diagram with staggered entrance and progressive line drawing — use for architectures, flows, relationships. NOT a card.
- `shape-morph-transition`: Cross-fade morph between two SVG shapes (square to star) with scale pulse — use for transformations, before/after. NOT a card.

**How to use:** Select templates by slug in scenes.json `"templates"` field. The Animator will receive their full source code.
If no template fits a scene, use an empty array — the Animator will create custom visuals.
